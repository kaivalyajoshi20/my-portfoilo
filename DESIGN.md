# CODEYAAR — Design direction

## Approved direction
- Character: industrial, energetic, experimental; confident and crafted rather than template-like.
- Base palette: graphite and charcoal with cool steel-gray surfaces.
- Primary accent: signal red for decisive actions and emphasis.
- Secondary accent: electric cyan used sparingly for interaction, focus and sparks.
- Typography: retain the existing Manrope display and DM Sans body pairing; use DM Mono only for compact technical metadata.
- Hero: no portrait in the hero; CODEYAAR wordmark remains the focal point.
- Signature interaction: a larger scratch-ticket panel with an ember edge, subtle cyan sparks and a clear reveal state.
- Motion: short, purposeful hover feedback; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Layout: keep content readable and responsive; avoid decorative elements that do not reinforce the studio's identity.

## Quality bar
- Every visual treatment should support hierarchy, navigation, content or interaction.
- Avoid generic gradient blobs, excessive uppercase micro-labels, redundant card grids and ornamental motion without purpose.
- Preserve accessibility, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts and the existing contact endpoint.
- Review desktop and mobile before considering the design complete.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026
This policy describes the website as currently operated. We will update it if our data practices change or applicable legal requirements require it.

1. Who operates this website

This website is operated under the name CODEYAAR by Kaivalya Joshi in India. For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact kaivalyaj2007@outlook.com.

2. Information you provide

If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, optional subject and message that you submit. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, health information or other sensitive personal information through the form.

3. Why we use it

We use enquiry information to read and respond to your message, discuss a possible project or collaboration, maintain relevant business correspondence, prevent abuse and protect the website. We do not sell enquiry information or use it for advertising.

4. Where information is processed

  • Cloudflare Pages and its infrastructure deliver the website and run its server-side contact endpoint. The infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL and security events.
  • Neon provides the database used to store contact-form submissions.
  • GitHub is contacted by your browser to display public repository information.
  • Google Fonts may be contacted by your browser to load the website's typefaces.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Their infrastructure may process technical information in countries other than India. We do not control their independent processing.

5. Retention and deletion

We aim to keep contact enquiries for no longer than 12 months after the last meaningful correspondence, unless a project is active or information must be retained for a legal obligation, dispute resolution or security purpose. Enquiries are reviewed and deleted manually; automatic deletion is not currently configured. You may request deletion by emailing us from the address associated with your enquiry. We may need to verify the request before acting.

6. Cookies and analytics

We do not currently operate advertising pixels or third-party analytics on this website, and we do not intentionally set marketing cookies. Our hosting or security provider may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms or cookies when required to deliver or protect the service. Third-party services you visit or your browser contacts may apply their own technologies under their own policies.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect submitted enquiries, including server-side validation, restricted administrative access, encrypted HTTPS connections and infrastructure security controls. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the contact form. Depending on applicable law and the commencement of relevant provisions, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data, withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis, or raise a grievance. Email kaivalyaj2007@outlook.com and describe your request. We will handle it under the laws that apply at the time.

9. Children

This website is a general portfolio and studio website and is not intended to invite children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the website, providers or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

Craft
over
clout.

Good software should feel intentional. Good design should have a reason to exist.

I’m interested in the space between how something works and how it feels to use.

My approach combines a developer’s instinct for structure with a designer’s eye for rhythm, hierarchy, and detail. I like interfaces that are clear without feeling generic, and engineering that makes the experience better rather than just more complicated.

This portfolio is a living work in progress — a place to share experiments, show how I think, and turn promising ideas into useful things. I’m always learning, refining, and looking for the next interesting problem.
